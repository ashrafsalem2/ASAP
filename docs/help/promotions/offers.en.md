# Offers

An offer is a rule that changes what a customer pays. ASAP ships five kinds, and most campaigns are one of them with a window on it.

**Percentage** — a share off the price. **Amount per unit** — a fixed sum off each one. **Buy X get Y** — the classic three-for-two, which is why a half-price-second-one needs no new code. **Threshold** — spend this much, get that off. **Fixed price** — this item is now this price.

Happy hour and coupons are windows and conditions on those rather than kinds of their own.

## Who gets it

An offer can be limited by branch, channel, coupon, date window, time of day and day of the week. A time window that ends before it starts crosses midnight — that is how a late-night offer is written, and reading it any other way would switch every one of them off.

## When two offers meet

The customer gets the best offer, and priority is only a tiebreak. An exclusive offer has to beat what the stackable ones would have come to together, or it is not the better deal. A blocking offer is settled before anything is worked out per line.

## What the discount does to the books

The discount posts to its own account, separate from the ordinary sales discount, and the receipt line records which offer priced it. A campaign can therefore be totalled from either end — by account, or by offer code — and the two agree.

## Before it runs

Every offer is priced against live cost when it is saved and again at every basket. See the margin topic: the check is the reason this module is worth having rather than a discount typed into a till.
