# Reversing a posting

Nothing posted is ever deleted or edited. A mistake is corrected by posting the mirror image of the original: every line, with the opposite sign.

## What a reversal leaves behind

The original entries stay exactly as they were posted — same amounts, same date, same description. What changes is that they now point at their correction, and the correction points back, so the pair reads from either end. Somebody looking at the account next year sees both, and can tell what happened rather than finding a gap.

## The date

A reversal is dated the same day as the original by default. That is what keeps a corrected month looking corrected, rather than pushing the fix into the next month and leaving both wrong. Where the original month has been closed, date the reversal in the current one — but then the correction is a prior-period adjustment and should be labelled as such.

## Reversing a reversal

Refused. A second reversal of the same transaction would double the correction rather than undo it, and on the account the two would look identical. If a reversal was itself wrong, reverse *it* — the correction of a correction is an ordinary entry like any other.
