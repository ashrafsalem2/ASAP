# Vendors

A vendor is a party with a ledger of its own. The shared ground with customers is in the finance topic on customers and vendors; this page is the purchasing side.

## Blocking

A vendor can be blocked for ordering, for posting, or for everything. Blocking for posting still lets somebody raise an order — useful where a supplier is under review but the goods are already agreed.

## What a vendor decides on a purchase

The payment terms, the tax treatment, the currency where the company trades in more than one, and the control account their balance lands in.

## Vendor invoice numbers

A vendor's own invoice number is recorded on the document. It is theirs, not yours, and it is what they will quote when they chase payment. Two invoices from one vendor with the same number is a warning rather than a refusal: duplicates happen, and so do vendors who restart their numbering every year.
