# The posting window

The posting window is the range of dates a user may post into. It is set at company level as policy and narrowed per user where it helps.

The usual arrangement is one everybody recognises: the controller can still post into last month while the close is finished, and the clerks cannot. Without it the choice is between leaving the month open for everybody — and finding an entry landed in it on the day the accounts were signed — or closing it and doing the remaining work yourself.

## It is not the same as closing a period

Closing a period stops everybody. The window is per user and moves without ceremony. Use the window for the fortnight either side of a month end, and close the period once the figures are agreed.

## What happens at the edge

A posting dated outside the window is refused, and the message says which date, whose window, and what the window is. Somebody holding the override permission may post anyway, and it goes to the audit log with their reason.
