# Promotions setup

Two settings, and one of them decides whether the module protects you or merely records what happened.

**The margin floor** is the least margin an offer may leave. See the margin topic; the short version is to set it to what the business cannot afford rather than to what it would prefer, because a floor that refuses ordinary trading gets overridden every day and then means nothing.

**The promotion discount account** is where offer discounts post, separate from the ordinary sales discount. Separate on purpose: a campaign totalled from the ledger and a campaign totalled from the receipt lines should agree, and they cannot if promotion discounts are mixed with the discount a salesperson negotiated.

## Numbering and codes

Offer codes appear on receipt lines and in the uptake report. Choose codes somebody will still recognise in a year — SUMMER26 rather than PROMO-14 — because the report is read long after the campaign is forgotten.
