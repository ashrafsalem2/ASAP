# Purchasing setup

**Goods received not invoiced** is the accrual account. It holds the value of stock that has arrived and not yet been billed, and it is the account that makes a balance sheet honest between the lorry and the paperwork. It should be near nothing at a month end; a balance that never moves usually means receipts nobody ever invoiced.

**Purchase price variance** is where the difference between the order price and the invoice price goes. Separate from cost on purpose: a supplier who quietly raises prices shows up in one account rather than in a margin that drifted for reasons nobody can name.

## Approval

Where an approval limit is set, an order above it needs somebody else to release it. Set it to the amount above which a second opinion is genuinely worth having. Set too low, everything needs approval and approval becomes a rubber stamp.

## Numbering

Purchase orders and vendor invoices come from series. Neither needs to be gapless — they are your documents, not tax documents, and the vendor's own invoice number is recorded separately.
