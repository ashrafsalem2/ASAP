# Point of sale setup

The settings here decide where a till's money goes and what a cashier may do without asking.

## Accounts

**Cash** is where the drawer's takings land. **Till differences** is where a short or over count goes. Both are written by the module rather than by hand, which is what keeps the cash account describing money that is actually in the building.

**Rounding** takes the fractions where the company rounds a total to a convenient amount. A separate account on purpose: rounding is a real cost or gain and burying it in revenue makes a margin that is quietly wrong.

## What a cashier may do

**The discount limit** is the most-used setting on this page. Above it, a refusal that a supervisor can push past with a reason, recorded in the audit log. Set it to what a cashier should be able to decide alone, not to what the largest legitimate discount is — the second makes the control meaningless.

**Whether a return needs the original receipt.** Requiring it prevents a return at list price against a discounted sale nobody can find. Not requiring it lets a shop be generous. Both are defensible; be sure which you have chosen.

## Numbering

Receipts are numbered per branch and gaplessly. See the tills topic.
