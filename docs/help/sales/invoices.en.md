# Sales invoices

An invoice is what commits a sale to the books. Until then an order is an intention.

## What it posts

Revenue at list price, the discount as a contra, tax on both, the customer's balance up by the total, and the stock out at what the goods cost. Exactly what a till receipt posts, so the profit and loss cannot tell which door a sale came through.

The invoice posts at the branch of the location the goods went out of — not at the branch of whoever keyed it. Otherwise every sale in the company reports against the person processing the paperwork.

## Tax

The tax code on a line decides the rate, and the rate that applies is the one in force on the invoice date rather than today's. That matters exactly once, when a rate changes, and it matters a great deal that day.

Tax is worked out on the revenue and on the discount, in the right direction. A discount reduces the taxable amount; treating it otherwise overstates the tax due on every discounted sale.

## Corrections

An invoice is never edited. A credit memo reverses what needs reversing, and both stay on the customer's ledger where somebody querying the account can see what happened.
