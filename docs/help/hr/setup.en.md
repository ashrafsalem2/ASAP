# Human resources setup

Four settings decide where the cost of employing people goes and what documents are numbered from.

**Wages and salaries** is where the cost of employing people is charged. It is debited per branch, so the shop that had somebody carries their wage for the days they were there. Branch performance reads this account to break staff cost out of expenses, so moving it without saying so empties that column.

**Net pay owed** is where what people are owed sits between the payroll being posted and the money leaving the bank. Two separate events, and treating them as one is how a company comes to believe it has paid staff it has not.

**End of service provision** is where the liability for what staff have earned is carried. What the company will owe when people leave is a liability now, not a cost that appears on the day somebody resigns.

**Unused leave provision** is the same idea for leave: days somebody has earned and not taken are owed to them in money on the day they leave.

## Numbering

Employee numbers and payroll run numbers each come from a series. The employee series is the one to think about: never reuse a leaver's number, because it would attach one person's service history to another and service history is what an end-of-service award is calculated from.
