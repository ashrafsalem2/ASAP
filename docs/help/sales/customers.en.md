# Customers

A customer is a party with a ledger of its own, a credit limit and a price agreement. The shared ground with vendors is covered in the finance topic on customers and vendors; this page is what the sales side adds.

## Blocking

A customer can be blocked for orders, for posting, or for everything. Blocking for posting still lets somebody quote and take an order — which is usually what you want while a payment is being chased, because refusing at the door loses the sale and refusing at the invoice loses nothing.

## Credit limits

The limit is checked when a document is posted, not when it is entered. The message says what they owe, what the limit is and by how much this document passes it. Somebody with the override permission may post anyway, with a reason, and it is recorded.

A limit of nothing means no limit, not a limit of zero. If you want a customer who may only pay cash, block them for posting.

## What a customer decides on a sale

The price agreement, the payment terms, the tax treatment, and the control account their balance lands in. Where they carry their own control account, their balance is shown separately on the balance sheet — worth doing rarely, because an account per customer makes a chart nobody can read.
